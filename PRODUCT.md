## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Recruiters and technical leads (Brazil and international) who decide in seconds whether to make contact, and freelance clients who want to understand what Cairo delivers. Both read the same page; recruiters first, freelance as a second reading.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Cairo Ramos, Full Stack Developer with 10+ years in production web systems. Success: a visitor sees real projects and measured results quickly and contacts Cairo by email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A developer who ships and operates full systems (APIs, queues, databases, front end, deploy) and shows them running, with modest claims backed by concrete numbers.

## Operating Context

Bilingual (PT-BR and EN) with a language toggle and light/dark themes. Hosted on GitHub Pages. Content comes from the V17 resume and public GitHub repositories.

- No phone number on the page. Contact: cairoramos7@gmail.com, LinkedIn, GitHub.

## Brand Commitments

Modest tone in all text. No self-congratulatory adjectives. Facts and numbers over adjectives.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public repos: image-hues (live demo at cairoramos7.github.io/image-hues), react-adapt-table, md2pdf, com.node-app.
- Production work: Nexhub (private code), Igreja Videira (videiraslmb.com).

## Product Principles

1. Show running work before describing it.
2. Numbers over adjectives.
3. Same truth in both languages.
4. Fast to read, light to load.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast in both themes, keyboard-reachable language and theme toggles, no motion that hides content.
